# Scoring

Where raters mark candidate speaking tests against the six ICAO language
descriptors. You'll only see tests that have been assigned to you.

## The six areas

Pronunciation, Structure, Vocabulary, Fluency, Comprehension, Interactions —
each scored 1–6. The **overall level is the lowest of the six** and is
worked out for you; you don't enter it.

## Marking a test

1. Open the assignment (from the Dashboard, or the Scoring tab).
2. Play the recording. Trainees see candidates anonymised as
   **Candidate A / B / C / D** rather than by name.
3. Enter a score for each of the six areas, plus any notes.
4. Use **Continue** to jump to the next candidate that still needs a score —
   it finds the next incomplete one regardless of order.
5. Your work saves automatically as you go and when you navigate away, so
   you can stop and come back.

## Reviewing and confirming

When every candidate has a score you get a **"Review your scores"** summary.
Nothing is final until you click **"Yes, that's my scores"** — that's the
point of no return:

- Before confirming: you can still change any score.
- After confirming: the test is locked, there's no way back into edit mode,
  and the admin is notified.

An amber "edited" marker shows next to any score you've changed since first
entering it — that's normal, it's just a reminder of what moved.
