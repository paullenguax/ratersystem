# Scoring

This is where raters mark candidate speaking tests against the six ICAO
language descriptors. You only see tests that have been assigned to you.

## The six areas

Pronunciation, Structure, Vocabulary, Fluency, Comprehension and
Interactions are each scored 1–6. The **overall level is the lowest of the
six**. It's worked out for you, so you don't enter it.

## Marking a test

1. Open **Scoring** and pick the assignment from **My Assignments**. If you
   came from the self-serve exam link, it opens for you.
2. Play the **Recording**. The speed buttons (0.75×, 1×, 1.25×, 1.5×)
   change the playback speed.
   - Trainees see the candidates as **Test A / B / C / D**, without names or
     test details.
   - Senior raters and admins see the candidate's name and test number.
3. Set each of the six areas with its slider or number box. Once an area
   has a score, hover the **ⓘ** next to it to read the ICAO descriptor for
   that level.
   - **Shortcut**: press the keys **1–6** to fill the next empty area.
   - If you try to move on with an area missing, it's marked "Please rate
     this criterion".
4. When all six are set, a green banner tells you which button saves it.
   Click **Continue to Candidate B** (or similar). This saves the test and
   moves to the next candidate that still needs a score, whatever order you
   took them in. On the last one, the button says **Complete**.
5. The **‹ ›** arrows step between candidates. Anything complete is saved
   on the way. Half-finished scores are kept while you stay on the page.

A green **scored** label shows on tests you've already saved. If you change
a saved answer, the banner turns amber and the button shows a pencil. That's
a reminder that you're overwriting an earlier score. Afterwards the test
shows **✓ your edit saved**.

## Reviewing and confirming

When every candidate has a score, you get a **Review your scores** summary.
Click a candidate to see all six scores. An amber **edited** marker shows
next to anything you changed after first scoring it. That's normal.

- **Review or change an answer** takes you back into the tests.
- **Yes, that's my scores** locks everything in. This is the point of no
  return:
  - Before confirming, you can still change any score.
  - After confirming, there's no way back into edit mode, and the admin is
    emailed to say you've finished.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Couldn't save your scores"** (red banner): usually a lost connection.
  Your scores are still on screen, so try the button again.
- **"No assignments pending"**: you have nothing to score. Either nothing
  has been assigned yet, or you've already confirmed it.
