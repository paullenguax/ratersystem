# Teaching a course  *(trainers)*

The **Teach** view is everything you need while running a session: the
plan, the audio to play, the students, and the materials. Open it with
**Teach** on a course's row in **Courses**, or on the course page.

## Layout

- **Day tabs** (rater courses): **Day 1** to **Day 5**, each showing how
  many of its recordings have been played (e.g. *3/8*). An **Added for this
  run** tab appears if recordings were added to this course by hand.
  Refresher courses have a single list.
- **Session plan** for the day, open at the top (see below).
- **Audio checklist** for the day.
- **Materials** for the day, then whole-course materials, then **General
  documents** (folded).
- **Students** down the side.

## Playing audio

- **▶** on a recording plays it through the player at the top of the page,
  which stays in view as you scroll. **0.75× / 1× / 1.25× / 1.5×** change
  the speed. Click **▶** again to pause.
- A recording **ticks itself off** when it plays to the end. You can also
  tick or untick it by hand, and **Untick all** resets the day.
- Ticks show the time they were made, and everyone viewing the course sees
  the same ticks. They're a progress record for the session only. Everyone
  on the course already counts as having heard its audio.

## Session plans

A session plan is a note that says what happens when: which recording to
play, what to discuss, breaks, homework. It shows **open at the top** of the
day it's for.

**Writing one:**

1. Go to **Courses → Materials & documents → Rater courses** (or
   **Refresher courses**), set the day, and click **New note**.
2. Write the plan. Refer to recordings by their Training number, such as
   **T12**:
   ```
   ## Day 2
   1. Recap the Day 1 descriptors (10 min)
   2. Play T14 and discuss pronunciation
   3. Compare T9 with T14: what changes in fluency?
   4. Break-out rooms: everyone scores T11, then compare
   ```
3. Click **Make plan** on the note (or tick **Session plan** in the
   editor).

**In the Teach view:**

- Each **T12** becomes a **▶ T12** button that plays the recording. It's
  highlighted while playing and turns **✓ T12** once played.
- The day's audio checklist follows the order the plan mentions recordings
  in. Anything the plan doesn't mention comes after.
- A number that isn't in this course's audio stays plain, with a dotted
  underline. Usually the recording hasn't been tagged for this course yet,
  or the number is mistyped.
- **Edit** on the plan opens it and brings you back to the same day.

A plan set to **Any day** shows on every day, which suits a whole-course
overview. For refreshers, write one plan with a heading for each day.

## Teaching notes on a recording

Points to bring up about a particular recording, written once and shown on
every course that plays it.

- Click the speech-bubble button on a recording's row. It's coloured when
  the recording has notes. The notes open under the row.
- **Add notes** / **Edit** to write them. Markdown works.
- Write a time as **m:ss** and it becomes a button that jumps the player to
  that point:
  ```
  0:42 — listen for the readback error
  1:58 — long pause before the numbers
  ```
- Notes show who last edited them. Admins can also edit them in **Test
  Bank** (the **Teaching notes** box on a Training test).

## Materials while teaching

- Files open in a new tab.
- Notes expand in place, already formatted. The printer icon prints one as a
  branded PDF, and **Edit** opens it.
- **General documents** at the bottom lists every general document, so
  standing documents are always to hand even if they aren't set to show on
  this course.
