# Practice Sessions  *(admin / trainer)*

Live scoring practice during a course. The trainer plays a recording to the
room; participants score it on their own devices; the trainer sees the
spread.

## Running one

1. **New session** — pick a recording. The picker only offers
   `standardization`‑category tests (rater‑course tests are reserved for
   the real end‑of‑course exam and must never be previewed in practice).
2. Share the join link (`/practice/<code>`). Participants can join with
   Canvas ("Continue with Canvas") or, if they must, a free‑text name.
3. The **trainer** plays the audio (there are 0.75×–1.5× speed controls on
   the trainer's results view); participants just score.
4. Review the spread. Two independent buttons: **Clear scores**, and
   **Save to standardization pool** (promotes the Canvas‑identified scores
   into Standardization — only enabled when the session is linked to a real
   recording and has at least one identified, not‑yet‑promoted score).

## Good to know

Scores from a free‑text (non‑Canvas) participant can never be promoted to
the standardization pool — this is deliberate.
