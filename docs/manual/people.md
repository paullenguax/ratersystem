# People  *(admin)*

The register of everyone who can use the system — raters, trainees, senior
raters, examiners, admins.

## Adding someone

- **Invite** (preferred): enter name, email and role. The system creates
  their account and emails them a link to set a password. Works for any
  role.
- **Canvas users**: usually you don't add them here at all — run
  **Admin → Canvas Sync** at the start of a course and it creates People
  records for every attendee from the course roster.

## Editing

Open a person to change their name, role, or status, or to tick
**Can standardize** (lets a senior rater or trainee into the
Standardization pipeline without making them an examiner).

## Good to know

- A person's record is tied to their login. Don't delete someone who has
  scored tests — set their status to inactive instead.
- Changing a role takes effect on their next sign‑in.
