# Sample Collection

A library of past test recordings kept for reference and practice —
listening to benchmarked samples to calibrate your ear.

## How to use it

Browse or filter the list, play a recording, and (where shown) see the
agreed scores for it. Nothing here is graded or recorded against you.

## Good to know

This draws from the same recordings as Test Bank but is read‑only and open
to trainees and senior raters, not just admins.
