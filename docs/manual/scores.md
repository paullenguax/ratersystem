# Scores  *(admin)*

The record of all **published** rater‑course scores. Read‑oriented: this is
where you look results up and export them.

## How to use it

- Filter by session, rater, test, etc.
- Export to CSV (there's also a Rasch‑formatted export used by the
  Statistics workflow).

## Good to know

Scores only appear here once the assignment has been **Published**. An
unpublished score is visible to its rater and on the Assignments review
page, but not here. Standardization scores are in **Standardization
Results**, not here.
